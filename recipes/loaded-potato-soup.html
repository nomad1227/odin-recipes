Homemade Loaded Potato Soup

Description

This homemade loaded potato soup is thick, creamy, and packed with tender potatoes, smoky bacon, cheddar cheese, and plenty of comforting flavor. It has everything you would expect from a loaded baked potato in a warm bowl of soup and is especially good on a cold evening.

  • 6 slices bacon, chopped
  • 2 pounds russet potatoes, peeled and diced
  • 1 whole medium yellow onion, chopped
  • 3 cloves garlic, minced
  • 3 Tbsp butter
  • ¼ cup all-purpose flour
  • 3 cups chicken broth
  • 2 cups whole milk
  • 1 cup heavy cream
  • 1½ cups shredded sharp cheddar cheese
  • ½ cup sour cream
  • ½ Tsp smoked paprika
  • Salt, to taste
  • Pepper, to taste
  • 3 green onions, sliced
  1. Cook the chopped bacon in a large pot over medium heat until crisp. Remove the bacon and set aside, leaving about 1-2 Tbsp of bacon grease in the pot.
  2. Add the chopped onion and cook until softened and lightly golden.
  3. Add the garlic and cook for about 30 seconds, stirring constantly.
  4. Add the butter. Once melted, sprinkle in the flour and stir continuously for about 1-2 minutes to remove the raw flour taste.
  5. Slowly pour in the chicken broth while stirring to prevent lumps from forming.
  6. Add the diced potatoes, smoked paprika, salt and pepper. Bring to a light boil, then reduce the heat and simmer for about 15-20 minutes, or until the potatoes are tender.
  7. Use a potato masher to lightly mash some of the potatoes directly in the pot. Leave plenty of potato pieces intact for texture.
  8. Stir in the milk and heavy cream. Continue cooking over low heat until the soup is hot, but do not allow it to rapidly boil.
  9. Add the shredded cheddar cheese a handful at a time, stirring until completely melted before adding more.
  10. Remove the soup from the heat and stir in the sour cream and about half of the cooked bacon.
  11. Serve topped with the remaining bacon, sliced green onions, additional cheddar cheese and a spoonful of sour cream, if desired.
