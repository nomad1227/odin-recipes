Spaghetti

Description

This classic spaghetti is a comforting, satisfying dish with tender pasta coated in a rich, savory tomato sauce. Warm, hearty, and full of familiar Italian-inspired flavor, it makes an easy meal that works just as well for a busy weeknight as it does for a relaxed family dinner.

  • 1 pound ground beef
  • 1 whole medium white onion, chopped
  • 3 cloves garlic, chopped
  • 2 Tbsp tomato paste
  • 2-28oz cans Hunt's® crushed tomatoes
  • 3 Tbsp worchestershire sauce
  • 1 Tsp dried oregano
  • 1 Tsp dried basil
  • Salt, to taste
  • Pepper, to taste
  • ½ Tsp crushed red pepper flakes
  • ⅛-¼ Tbsp butter (not margarine), optional
  • A few fresh basil leaves, sliced into strips
  1. Brown ground beef in a deep skillet. Drain and set aside.
  2. Sauté the onion until golden brown. Add the garlic about halfway, so the garlic does not burn. No need to completely cook these right now. They will cook completely later.
  3. Add tomato paste and combine with onions and garlic.
  4. Add ground beef back to the pan and add the two cans of crushed tomatoes.
  5. Add dried spices, salt, pepper and worchestershire sauce.
  6. Bring to a light boil, then reduce heat to very low. Simmer low for 45 minutes to an hour and a half, stirring occasionally. The longer you simmer, the better the sauce will combine and the better the flavor you will get from the sauce. Do not rush this step.
  7. About 15 minutes before you want to serve, add butter to the sauce and stir as it melts. This is optional, but the dairy fat will reduce the acidity of the sauce caused by the tomatoes and enrich the sauce.
  8. After removing the sauce from the heat, att the strips of fresh basil to give the sauce that fresh, iconic italian flavor.
