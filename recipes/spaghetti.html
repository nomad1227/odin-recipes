Spaghetti

Description

This classic spaghetti is a comforting, satisfying dish with tender pasta coated in a rich, savory tomato sauce. Warm, hearty, and full of familiar Italian-inspired flavor, it makes an easy meal that works just as well for a busy weeknight as it does for a relaxed family dinner.

  • 1 pound ground beef
  • 1 whole medium white onion, chopped
  • 3 cloves garlic, chopped
  • 2 Tbsp tomato paste
  • 2-28oz cans Hunt's® crushed tomatoes
  • 3 Tbsp worchestershire sauce
  • 1 Tsp dried oregano
  • 1 Tsp dried basil
  • Salt, to taste
  • Pepper, to taste
  • ½ Tsp crushed red pepper flakes
  • ⅛-¼ Tbsp butter (not margarine)
  • A few fresh basil leaves, sliced into strips
