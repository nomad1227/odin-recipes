Honey Garlic Pork Chops

Description

These honey garlic pork chops are juicy, savory, and coated in a sticky homemade sauce with the perfect balance of sweetness, garlic, and a little tang. They come together quickly in one skillet and have enough flavor to feel like more than just another simple weeknight dinner.

  • 4 boneless pork chops, about 1 inch thick
  • 1 Tbsp olive oil
  • 2 Tbsp butter
  • 4 cloves garlic, minced
  • ¼ cup honey
  • 3 Tbsp soy sauce
  • 1 Tbsp apple cider vinegar
  • 1 Tsp Dijon mustard
  • ½ Tsp smoked paprika
  • ½ Tsp garlic powder
  • Salt, to taste
  • Pepper, to taste
  • 1 Tbsp fresh parsley, chopped
  1. Pat the pork chops dry with paper towels and season both sides with smoked paprika, garlic powder, salt and pepper.
  2. Heat olive oil in a large skillet over medium-high heat.
  3. Add the pork chops and cook for about 3-4 minutes per side, until nicely browned. Remove them from the skillet and set aside.
  4. Reduce the heat to medium and add the butter and minced garlic. Cook for about 30 seconds, stirring constantly so the garlic does not burn.
  5. Add the honey, soy sauce, apple cider vinegar and Dijon mustard. Stir until everything is well combined.
  6. Let the sauce simmer for 2-3 minutes, stirring occasionally, until it begins to thicken slightly.
  7. Return the pork chops to the skillet and spoon the sauce over the top.
  8. Continue cooking for another 3-5 minutes, flipping the pork chops once and coating both sides with the sauce, until the pork reaches an internal temperature of 145°F.
  9. Remove the skillet from the heat and let the pork chops rest for about 3 minutes.
  10. Spoon the remaining honey garlic sauce over the pork chops and sprinkle with fresh parsley before serving.
